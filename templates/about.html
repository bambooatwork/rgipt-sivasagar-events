About RGIPT Sivasagar Campus

The Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT) was established by an Act of Parliament (RGIPT Act 2007) under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, Government of India. Its Sivasagar Campus in Assam carries that mission into India's oil-rich North-East.

Our story

A hub for petroleum & energy education

RGIPT is a domain-specific institution co-promoted by six leading oil Public Sector Units — ONGC, IOCL, OIL, GAIL, BPCL and HPCL — in association with the Oil Industry Development Board (OIDB). The Sivasagar centre was established in 2016 to supplement academic activities and offer diploma and training programmes in the petroleum and natural gas domain. It was historically known as the Assam Energy Institute (AEI), Sivasagar.

Situated in the upper reaches of the Brahmaputra Valley, the campus sits amid extensive oilfield activity and gives students direct exposure to the industry, supported by Oil India Limited (OIL), ONGC and leading research and training institutes.

Programmes & admissions

Diploma programmes

The campus runs six industry-oriented diploma programmes with 80 seats each — 480 seats in total.

Fee waiver: 100% tuition waiver for students securing 95% and above in the Class 10 board examination.

Lateral entry: Diploma graduates with a CPI of 7.5 or above and no active backlog are eligible for direct admission to the B.Tech programme at RGIPT Jais Campus.

Seat reservation: 50% of seats are reserved for Assam and 50% for the rest of India. The campus also works towards 20% girl students; PwD reservation is as per rules.

History: Established in 2016 as a centre of RGIPT; the Petroleum Engineering branch was set up in 2017 — the first of its kind in Assam to offer a Diploma in Petroleum Engineering.

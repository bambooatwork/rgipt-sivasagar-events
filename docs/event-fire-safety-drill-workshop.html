About this event

A hands-on fire and industrial safety workshop for students of Fire & Safety Engineering and allied branches. Sessions cover fire chemistry, types and use of extinguishers, breathing apparatus, rescue techniques and mock evacuation drills, delivered by certified safety instructors in partnership with industry.

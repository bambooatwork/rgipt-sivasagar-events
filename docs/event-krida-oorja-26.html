About this event

Krida Oorja is the much-loved annual sports festival of RGIPT Sivasagar Campus, celebrating teamwork, sportsmanship and healthy competition. The 2026 edition spans three weeks of cricketing rivalries, football, volleyball, badminton, athletics and indoor games, opening with the traditional torch-lighting ceremony and March Past. Departments battle it out for the overall championship trophy, culminating in a grand closing ceremony with guest dignitaries.

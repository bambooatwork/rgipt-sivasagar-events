Industry Connect: OIL Field Visit & Expert Seminar

A guided field visit and seminar with Oil India Limited (OIL) engineers on upstream operations in the Assam oil belt.

About this event

RGIPT Sivasagar Campus sits in the heart of Assam's oil-rich belt, giving students unparalleled exposure to the petroleum industry. This Industry Connect session combines a guided field visit with a technical seminar led by engineers from Oil India Limited (OIL) and the Department of Petroleum Engineering. Topics include upstream exploration & production, well logging, enhanced oil recovery and HSE practices in the North-East fields.
